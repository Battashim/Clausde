Measure with your feet · plan by headcount

Pitch planner

Every size in this app is in metres, like “20 × 20 m”: 20 metres long and 20 metres wide (400 m² in total). You don't need a tape. Measure your step once, and the app turns every size into a number of steps.

1 · Measure your stepWalk a distance you know, normally, and count your steps. The app divides the distance by your steps.
2 · Pace out the gridCone, walk one side, cone, turn 90°, repeat. Then walk both diagonals: if they match, the corners are square.
3 · Size it to your groupPick the number of players and the type of activity. You get the size in metres and in your steps.

Step 1 · Your step length

Calibrate your step

Walk normally, as you would when setting out cones. Don't stretch. Use pitch markings from a full-size (11v11) pitch; youth pitches have smaller boxes.

Average adult walking step: about 0.75–0.8 m. A big, deliberate stride is about 1 m, so “1 big step = 1 metre” works if you stride.

Step 3 · Plan by headcount and space

How big should my area be?

Pick how many players are in the activity and what it's for. Tighter spaces mean more pressure and quicker decisions; bigger spaces mean more running and more time on the ball.

What we borrowed, what we built

The Pitchcraft method

Coerver gives players the ball skills. Ekkono teaches them when and why to use those skills. Fàbregas gives the team a brave, possession-first style. Pitchcraft puts both into every drill and makes it competitive, so players enjoy it and keep coming back.

The player development pyramid

Influence 2 · Ekkono Method

Game intelligence first

Technique always serves a decision. Players learn to perceive, decide and then execute, in situations that look like the real game.

PerceiveScan before the ball arrives: teammates, opponents, space.
DecidePass, dribble, shoot, hold. The coach asks questions instead of giving answers.
ExecuteTechnique under real pressure, with direction and opponents.
SuperiorityCreate 2v1s and 3v2s: in numbers, in position, or in quality.
SituationsTrain 1v1, 2v1, rondos and positional games before big games.
PhasesAttack, defend and both transitions are coached as one game.

Influence 3 · Cesc Fàbregas

Brave possession, played between the lines

As a player, Fàbregas was a playmaker for Arsenal, Barcelona and Chelsea, known for scanning and the final pass. As a coach (head coach of Como since 2024) his teams are known for building from the keeper, controlling the ball, and winning it back fast. We took six ideas from that style.

Brave build-upThe keeper and centre-backs play through the press. Long balls are a choice, not an escape.
Inside to go outsideFullbacks step into midfield to create a box, which frees the winger.
Live in the pocket8s and 10s receive between the opponent's lines, half-turned, facing goal.
Scan like a playmakerLook before the ball arrives so the next pass is already decided.
5-second huntLose it, win it back immediately, before the opponent can counter.
Trust young playersTechnical, brave young players get minutes and responsibility.

Our creation

4 Pitchcraft rules

1Every rep has a decisionNo drill without a read. Cones become defenders, gates become choices, coach cards become scanning cues.
2Touches before talkingAim for 1,000+ touches a session. Keep queues short: max 2 players waiting per line.
3Score everythingPoints, bonus points, streaks, a weekly leaderboard. Bonus points always reward the behaviour you coach.
4Same game, own roleDefenders, midfielders, attackers and keepers play the same activity, each with a position-specific job and scoring.
Session shape · used in the D & C License

Play – Practice – Play

15–20 minPlayPlayers arrive and play a small-sided game right away. You watch and spot the problem.
30–45 minPracticeA mastery block (Coerver) and then a situation (Ekkono) that fixes that one problem.
20–30 minPlayThe big game again, with a Pitchcraft bonus for the behaviour you trained. You check if it transfers.
The language of every license

The 4 moments of the game

In possessionBuild up, progress, create, finish. Width, depth, support.
Lost the ballTransition to defend: counter-press for 5 seconds or drop into shape.
Out of possessionPressure, cover, balance. Stay compact, show them away from goal.
Won the ballTransition to attack: play forward fast or keep it safe and rebuild.
