What we borrowed, what we built

The Pitchcraft method

Coerver gives players the ball skills. Ekkono teaches them when and why to use those skills. Pitchcraft puts both into every drill and makes it competitive, so players enjoy it and keep coming back.

Influence 2 · Ekkono Method

Game intelligence first

Technique always serves a decision. Players learn to perceive, decide and then execute, in situations that look like the real game.

PerceiveScan before the ball arrives: teammates, opponents, space.
DecidePass, dribble, shoot, hold. The coach asks questions instead of giving answers.
ExecuteTechnique under real pressure, with direction and opponents.
SuperiorityCreate 2v1s and 3v2s: in numbers, in position, or in quality.
SituationsTrain 1v1, 2v1, rondos and positional games before big games.
PhasesAttack, defend and both transitions are coached as one game.
Our creation

4 Pitchcraft rules

1Every rep has a decisionNo drill without a read. Cones become defenders, gates become choices, coach cards become scanning cues.
2Touches before talkingAim for 1,000+ touches a session. Keep queues short: max 2 players waiting per line.
3Score everythingPoints, bonus points, streaks, a weekly leaderboard. Bonus points always reward the behaviour you coach.
4Same game, own roleDefenders, midfielders, attackers and keepers play the same activity, each with a position-specific job and scoring.
Session shape · used in the D & C License

Play – Practice – Play

15–20 minPlayPlayers arrive and play a small-sided game right away. You watch and spot the problem.
30–45 minPracticeA mastery block (Coerver) and then a situation (Ekkono) that fixes that one problem.
20–30 minPlayThe big game again, with a Pitchcraft bonus for the behaviour you trained. You check if it transfers.
The language of every license

The 4 moments of the game

In possessionBuild up, progress, create, finish. Width, depth, support.
Lost the ballTransition to defend: counter-press for 5 seconds or drop into shape.
Out of possessionPressure, cover, balance. Stay compact, show them away from goal.
Won the ballTransition to attack: play forward fast or keep it safe and rebuild.
